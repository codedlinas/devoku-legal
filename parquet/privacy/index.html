Privacy Policy

Devoku Parquet · Last updated: February 9, 2026

Overview

Devoku Parquet is a desktop application for viewing Apache Parquet files. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Data Collection

Devoku Parquet does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No Analytics: We do not track how you use the app.
  • No File Access: Your parquet files are processed locally on your device only.
  • No Network Transmission: Your data never leaves your computer.
  • No Accounts: No login or registration is required.
  • No Cookies: The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Network Access

The app requires network access solely to download the DuckDB WebAssembly runtime from official CDN servers on first launch. No user data is transmitted during this process.

Data Storage

All files you open are processed entirely on your local machine. We have no access to your files or their contents.

Third-Party Services

Devoku Parquet does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
