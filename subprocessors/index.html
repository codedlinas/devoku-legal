Sub-processors

Devoku · Last updated: August 3, 2026

This page lists the third-party service providers ("sub-processors") that
Coded, UAB uses to provide the managed Devoku Cloud
service, and the data they may process. It is referenced by the
Privacy Policy and the
https://legal.devoku.com/subprocessors/.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Self-hosted and local-only deployments do not use Devoku-operated sub-processors
except those you configure yourself.

Infrastructure sub-processors

Provider Function Data processed Location EU (primary)
Hetzner Virtual machine hosting / compute Workloads and content processed on your VMs Confirm
UpCloud Virtual machine hosting / compute Workloads and content processed on your VMs Confirm
Cloudflare (R2) Object storage (files, backups, images) Uploaded files, backups Confirm

Platform / functionality sub-processors

Provider Function Data processed
Soniox Speech-to-text transcription of voice messages Voice recordings you submit
Voyage AI Text embeddings for search/memory Text content processed for embeddings
Resend Transactional email (verification, recovery, notifications) Email address, message content
Apple Push Notification service (APNs) iOS/macOS push delivery Device push token, notification content
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Android push delivery Device push token, notification content
Browser push services (via Web Push / VAPID) Browser/PWA push delivery Push endpoint/keys, notification content
Stripe (planned: Stripe) Payment processing for credit top-ups Payment metadata (no full card numbers stored by Devoku)

Not a sub-processor: Identity/authentication uses self-hosted Ory Kratos
software running on Devoku's own infrastructure. Credentials are stored in Devoku's
database; Ory (the company) does not receive your data.

AI / model providers (enabled by you)

When you enable AI features, your instructions, prompts, and relevant workspace
content are transmitted to the tools and model providers you choose so they can
perform the requested task. Depending on your configuration this may include:

Category Examples
Coding tools Cursor, Claude Code, Codex CLI, Qwen Code, Cline
Model providers OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, and others you configure

These providers act under their own terms and privacy policies. Where this routing is
optional, it is governed by the third_party_ai_providers consent category.

Notes for maintaining this list

  • Keep this list in sync with the Privacy Policy sub-processor table and the DPA Annex 3.
  • When adding or replacing a sub-processor, follow the notice/objection process in the DPA
    (30 days).
  • Firebase is used for push messaging only (FCM), not analytics or authentication.
  • Ory Kratos is self-hosted software, not a third-party processor (see note above).
